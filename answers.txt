Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science can be applied to Marketing. This is because it will help the team
better understand what demographics they are hitting and where they need to focus on. 
For example, Tik Tok should use data science to see what age range buys the most on Tik Tok
shop. This way they can show more ads for that age range and produce more revenue.
